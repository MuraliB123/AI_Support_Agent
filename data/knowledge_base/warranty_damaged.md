# Warranty & Damaged Item Policy

**Policy ID:** `WARRANTY-DAMAGE`  
**Brand:** Nimbus Home

## Standard Warranty

Nimbus Home products include a **1-year limited warranty** from delivery date against manufacturing defects.

Warranty covers:

- Defective materials or workmanship
- Dead-on-arrival (DOA) units
- Failure under normal household use

Warranty does **not** cover:

- Accidental damage, misuse, or commercial use
- Normal wear (e.g., non-stick coating after 12+ months)
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers

## Damaged on Arrival (DOA)

Report within **7 days** of delivery.

**Required evidence:**

1. Photo of shipping label on box
2. Photo of outer box damage (if visible)
3. Photo of product damage
4. Photo of serial number label (if applicable)

**Resolution (customer choice):**

- Free replacement shipped priority, OR
- Full refund including shipping (no return required for DOA under $150)
- Items over $150: return label provided; refund after scan or immediate replacement at agent discretion

## Damaged in Transit

See `SHIP-SLA` — same photo requirements within **48 hours** of delivery.

## Manufacturing Defect (After 7 Days)

Within warranty period:

1. Troubleshooting steps per product FAQ (if applicable)
2. If unresolved, request photos/video of defect
3. Offer repair (rare), replacement, or pro-rated refund based on age of product

| Product age | Typical resolution |
| --- | --- |
| 0–30 days | Replacement preferred |
| 31–180 days | Replacement or 80% refund |
| 181–365 days | 50% refund or replacement (agent discretion) |

## Missing Parts

If accessories are missing from box (not full item missing — see shipping):

- Ship missing parts at no charge if confirmed against packing slip
- If parts unavailable, offer **15% partial refund** or full return under `REFUND-14DAY`

## Serial Number Verification

For warranty claims over **$200**, verify serial number against order record before approving replacement.
