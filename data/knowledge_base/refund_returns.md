# Refund & Returns Policy

**Policy ID:** `REFUND-14DAY`  
**Brand:** Nimbus Home  
**Last updated:** January 1, 2026

## Overview

Nimbus Home offers a **14-day return window** from the date of delivery for most eligible items. Returns must be approved before items are shipped back. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after we receive and inspect the return.

## Eligibility

Returns are eligible when **all** of the following are true:

- The item was delivered within the last **14 calendar days**
- The item is unused, in original packaging, and includes all accessories
- The return is not excluded under **Non-Returnable Items** (below)
- An **RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization)** number has been issued

To start a return, the customer must provide: **order number**, **item SKU**, and **reason for return**.

## RMA Process

1. Customer requests return via support ticket or the Returns portal
2. Agent verifies eligibility against `REFUND-14DAY`
3. If approved, issue RMA number (format: `RMA-XXXXXX`)
4. Customer ships item back using the prepaid label (if provided)
5. Warehouse inspects within **3–5 business days** of receipt
6. Refund processed within **5–7 business days** after approval

## Restocking Fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to returns of opened but resellable items in these categories:

- Small appliances (kettles, blenders, air fryers)
- Bedding sets (if opened)

No restocking fee for defective items or items damaged in transit (see `WARRANTY-DAMAGE`).

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned under `REFUND-14DAY`:

- Personalized or custom-engraved products
- Hygiene products (pillows, mattress toppers) once opened
- Final sale / clearance items marked **FINAL-SALE**
- Gift cards

## Refund Timing

| Payment method | Refund timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Credit / debit card | 5–7 business days after inspection |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days after inspection |
| Store credit (if offered) | Immediate upon approval |

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may apply when:

- Item is returned without original packaging
- Item shows signs of use beyond inspection
- Missing accessories (deduction per missing item, up to 20% of item value)

## Escalation

Escalate to a senior agent if:

- Customer is outside the 14-day window but claims delivery delay (check tracking)
- Order value exceeds **$500**
- Customer mentions chargeback or legal action
