# General FAQ — Nimbus Home

**Policy ID:** `FAQ-GENERAL`  
**Brand:** Nimbus Home

Quick answers for common questions. For detailed rules, see linked policy IDs.

## Orders & Tracking

**Q: Where is my order?**  
A: Check your tracking link in the shipping confirmation email, or visit **Track Order** with your order number and email. Standard delivery is 5–7 business days (continental US). See `SHIP-SLA`.

**Q: Can I change my order after placing it?**  
A: Only while status is `pending` or `processing` (before ship). See `ORDER-MODIFY`.

**Q: I didn't receive a confirmation email.**  
A: Check spam/junk. Confirm the email address entered at checkout. If still missing, contact support with full name and payment last-4 for lookup.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: What is your return window?**  
A: **14 days** from delivery for eligible items. Policy ID: `REFUND-14DAY`.

**Q: How do I get an RMA number?**  
A: Open a return request with your order number and reason. Once approved, you'll receive an RMA number (format `RMA-XXXXXX`).

**Q: When will I get my refund?**  
A: 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the return (card payments). See `REFUND-14DAY` refund timing table.

## Shipping

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**  
A: US and Canada. See `SHIP-SLA` for delivery estimates.

**Q: My package is late — what can you do?**  
A: If delivery is 3+ business days past estimate (and not due to weather/address issues), you may qualify for store credit per `SHIP-SLA`.

**Q: My item arrived damaged.**  
A: Send photos within 48 hours (box + product). See `WARRANTY-DAMAGE`.

## Payments & Promos

**Q: Why was I charged twice?**  
A: Often a pending authorization plus capture — usually resolves in 3–5 days. True duplicates are investigated per `PAY-BILLING`.

**Q: Can I use two promo codes?**  
A: Generally no — one code per order unless marked `STACKABLE`. See `PROMO-CODES`.

**Q: My promo code doesn't work.**  
A: Check expiration and restrictions (minimum order, new customer only, excluded categories). Code `WELCOME10` is first-order only.

## Account

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page. Link expires in 24 hours. See `ACCOUNT-ACCESS`.

**Q: How do I update my email?**  
A: Log in → Account Settings → Email. Confirmation required on new address. See `ACCOUNT-ACCESS`.

## Products

**Q: What warranty do Nimbus Home products have?**  
A: **1-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects. See `WARRANTY-DAMAGE`.

**Q: Are your products UL/ETL certified?**  
A: Electrical items are certified per listing on product page. Direct specific certification questions to product team if not on PDP.

## Contact

- **Support hours:** Mon–Fri 9 AM – 6 PM ET; Sat 10 AM – 4 PM ET
- **Email:** support@nimbushome.example
- **Response target:** first reply within **24 hours** (business days)
