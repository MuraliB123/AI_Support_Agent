# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Policy ID:** `SHIP-SLA`  
**Brand:** Nimbus Home

## Standard Shipping

| Region | Carrier | Estimated delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continental US | UPS Ground / FedEx Ground | 5–7 business days |
| Alaska / Hawaii | USPS Priority | 10–14 business days |
| Canada | Canada Post / UPS | 7–12 business days |

Orders placed before **2:00 PM ET** on business days ship the same day. Orders after cutoff ship the next business day.

## Express Shipping

- **Express (2–3 business days):** available at checkout for $14.99 flat rate (continental US)
- **Overnight:** available for select SKUs only; quote at checkout

Express shipping fees are **non-refundable** unless we fail to meet the quoted delivery window.

## Tracking

Every shipped order receives a tracking number via email within **24 hours** of dispatch. Customers can track at `track.nimbushome.example` or via the carrier link.

If tracking shows **no movement for 5+ business days**, open a shipping investigation (see Late Delivery).

## Late Delivery

If delivery exceeds the estimated window by **3 or more business days** (and the delay is not due to weather, carrier strike, or incorrect address):

- Offer a **$10 store credit** for orders under $100
- Offer a **$25 store credit** for orders $100–$299
- Offer **$50 store credit or partial refund of shipping** for orders $300+

Do **not** promise full order refund for late delivery unless the package is confirmed lost (see Lost Packages).

## Lost Packages

A package is considered lost when:

- Tracking shows delivered but customer denies receipt (after 48h investigation), OR
- No tracking updates for **10 business days** after last scan

Resolution path:

1. File carrier claim (internal — do not ask customer to contact carrier first)
2. Offer **reship** (preferred) or **full refund** if item is out of stock

## Address Changes

Address changes are allowed **only before the order ships**. Once shipped, redirect via carrier (fees may apply) or wait for return-to-sender.

## Damaged in Transit

If the box arrives damaged, customer must provide **photos of outer box and product** within **48 hours** of delivery. Route to `WARRANTY-DAMAGE` for replacement or refund — no return shipping required for clearly transit-damaged goods.
